## Example Business Use Cases

Potential applications include:

### Facilities Monitoring

Monitor:

- HVAC systems
- Refrigeration
- Equipment rooms
- Pumps
- Compressors
- Environmental conditions

### Agriculture

Monitor:

- Greenhouses
- Soil conditions
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Irrigation systems

### Manufacturing

Monitor:

- Equipment temperature
- Machine state
- Vibration
- Production telemetry
- Preventive-maintenance indicators

### Small Business Infrastructure

Provide low-cost remote equipment monitoring and alerting for organizations
that do not need a large enterprise monitoring platform.
